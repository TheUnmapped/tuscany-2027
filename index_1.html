The plan

Your eight days, day by day

  1. 1Sat, May 22

    Welcome to Chianti

    A private driver picks you up at Florence airport and takes you to the villa. Settle in, then join the welcome dinner.

  2. 2Sun, May 23

    San Gimignano and a winery

    Explore a famous hilltop town. Then enjoy a country lunch with wine and olive oil tasting, and tour a winery.

  3. 3Mon, May 24

    Villa day

    Sleep in. Swim in the pool. Read in the sun. Tonight, a guest chef from the group cooks dinner.

  4. 4Tue, May 25

    Cooking class

    Learn to cook Italian food near the old Badia a Passignano abbey. Then eat what you made, with wine.

  5. 5Wed, May 26

    Free day

    Rest at the villa or visit the little villages nearby. You choose.

  6. 6Thu, May 27

    Val d'Orcia wine day

    Ride through Montepulciano, Pienza, and Montalcino. Visit Avignonesi Winery for a big lunch and eight wines.

  7. 7Fri, May 28

    Photo shoot and farewell

    A pro photographer spends two hours with the group at the villa. Then share one last dinner together.

  8. 8Sat, May 29

    Arrivederci

    A private driver takes you back to Florence airport.

Questions

Things people ask

Who is this trip for?

It is for friends who want to relax, eat well, and drink good wine together. If you love good food and slow days in the sun, you will love this trip.

How much does it cost?

Villa Beltrude is $4,500 per person. Villa Jobaldo is $5,900 per person. Both prices are based on 10 guests sharing the villa.

Are flights included?

No. You book your own flight into Florence. Once you land, a private driver takes you to the villa, and back to the airport at the end.

Do I have to cook?

Only one night. Each pair or small group cooks one favorite Italian dish for everyone. You pick your night and dish after you book. Other nights are light suppers or planned dinners.

What if I have food allergies?

Just tell Ronda after you book. She will share your needs with the group and the cooks.

When do I pay?

Book by the first week of January 2027. Your final payment is due March 31, 2027. Ronda will go over the details on your call.

Who plans this trip?

The Unmapped plans it with Travelive, a luxury travel partner in Italy. Ronda Walker hosts the trip in person from start to finish.

How many people can go?

Only 10. Spots go in the order people book.
